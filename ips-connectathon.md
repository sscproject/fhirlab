# IPS Connectathon Toolkit

The International Patient Summary (IPS) is a short, standard summary of a patient's health: allergies, medicines, problems, immunisations and more. It means a patient's key information can follow them across systems and borders.

A connectathon is where teams bring their systems together and test that they really can exchange this information. The teams that get the most out of it are the ones that show up prepared.

This toolkit gives country teams a free, shared place to practise beforehand. You can learn the IPS, try your ideas, find and fix problems early, and arrive at the connectathon confident in what you've built.

# The tools

## IPS FHIR Server

**[ips.fhirlab.net](https://ips.fhirlab.net/){:target="_blank"}**

A working FHIR R4 server set up for IPS. Your team can use it as a shared practice space.

- Load test patients and their allergies, medicines, conditions and immunisations.
- Build IPS documents and see how the pieces fit together.
- Point your own system at it to practise sending and receiving IPS data.
- Explore together. Nobody can break anything that matters, so it's a safe place for new team members to learn by doing.

## Inferno IPS Test Kit

**[inferno.fhirlab.net/ips](https://inferno.fhirlab.net/ips/){:target="_blank"}**

Inferno is an open testing tool that checks your work against the IPS Implementation Guide (v1.1.0).

- Paste in a single IPS bundle and see straight away what's right and what needs fixing.
- Run the full test suite against your own FHIR server.
- Get clear, specific feedback, so the team knows exactly what to work on next.
- Re-run the tests as you improve, and watch your results get better.

# How to prepare as a team

1. **Learn together.** Get the team familiar with the IPS and what goes into a patient summary. Our [Learning Activities](../learning) are a good place to start.
2. **Build and explore.** Use the IPS FHIR Server to create test patients and IPS documents. Share what you find with each other.
3. **Test and improve.** Check your bundles and your server with Inferno. Fix the issues, then test again.
4. **Arrive ready.** Go into the connectathon knowing your system works, with a team that has learned and grown together.
